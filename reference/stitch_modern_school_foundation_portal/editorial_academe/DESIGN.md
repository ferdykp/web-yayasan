## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, prestige-driven educational foundation ethos. It merges classical academic authority with a contemporary, humanistic warmth inspired by high-end independent magazines and modern institution portals.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Scholarly & Forward-Looking:** Balances generational credibility with modern pedagogy.
- **Nurturing & Wholesome:** Evokes safety, natural growth, character building, and emotional well-being.
- **Prestige Editorial:** Replaces clinical institutional styling with dynamic storytelling, expressive display serifs, curated photography, and refined typographic hierarchy.

### Design Movement
- **Modern Academic Editorial:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise geometric sans-serif UI elements.
- **Tactile Botanical Nuance:** Deep biophilic greens, soft warm cream paper backdrops, organic leaf badge silhouettes, and subtle curved borders that mimic printed annuals and journals.

## Colors

The palette establishes grounded academic trust through deep botanical tones layered over warm paper neutrals, accented by vivacious vegetative greens.

### Primary Role & Tonal Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#174C3C` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors primary call-to-actions, brand identity, navigation backdrops, key highlights, and primary typographic weight.
- **Secondary (`#6F927D` - Muted Sage):** Used for supporting UI lines, quiet borders, secondary button outlines, tab selectors, and muted instructional metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#C4DC75` - Lime Accent):** High-energy visual spark reserved for active conversion triggers (e.g., PPDB admission badges, status pills, floating badges, and notification tags).
- **Neutral Deep (`#10382D` - Dark Forest):** Deepest contrast level used for immersive inverted sections, high-density text, and dark institutional footers.

### Surfaces & Background Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#F6F4EC` - Warm Cream):** Natural editorial paper tone that eliminates clinical digital glare and softens photography boundaries.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Bright card face used for tiered cards, content containers, and form fields.
- **Surface Muted (`#E5EFE7` - Light Mint Tint):** Subtle card fills, table headers, icon badge containers, and hover states.

## Typography

The typographic pairing provides deliberate tension between authoritative warmth and functional precision.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Display & Headings (Newsreader):** Renders editorial sophistication with prominent optical sizing, fluid serifs, and high stroke contrast. It should be typeset in sentence case for headlines. It supports italic styling specifically for poetic callouts, editorial annotations, and pull quotes.
- **Interface & Body (Manrope):** A geometric grotesque with humanist open apertures. Provides utmost legibility in small captions, metadata, statistics numbers, and navigation links.
- **Eyebrows & Section Markers:** Uses `label-sm` or `label-md` with strict uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to act as clean architectural index markers above serif section headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs an editorial grid system designed for asymmetric visual balance, generous breathing space, and structured photo arrays.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px - 1440px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Maximum container width capped at `1320px` for optimal reading flow.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Multi-column cards compress to 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Asymmetric heroes collapse into stacked editorial units with sticky bottom CTAs.

### Layout Rhythms
- **Vertical Flow:** Section-to-section gaps adhere to an expansive scale (`5rem` to `7rem` on desktop, `3.5rem` on mobile) to maintain an elevated, magazine-like cadence.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Photography blocks intentionally bleed across grid columns or anchor absolute editorial badges that overlap card boundaries by `1.5rem` to break boxy rigidity.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system minimizes synthetic drop shadows in favor of tonal paper layering, subtle botanical borders, and high-clarity floating elements.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Layer:** Warm Cream (`#F6F4EC`) provides the base paper foundation.
- **Card Tier:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Light Mint (`#E5EFE7`) resting upon the cream background with a hair-thin border (`1px solid rgba(23, 76, 60, 0.08)`).
- **Hero Cutouts & Overlays:** Photographic subjects cut out against geometric cream/mint backplates create an architectural sense of foreground, midground, and background.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
- **Default / Flat Cards:** Zero blur shadow; separation is achieved entirely through outline stroke (`#174C3C` at 8% opacity) and surface color shift.
- **Elevated Editorial Chips / Floating Badges:** Low-opacity, warm-tinted diffused shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 56, 45, 0.08)`.
- **Active Modals / Mobile Drawer:** Deep ambient shade: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(16, 56, 45, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates full pill profiles for interactive triggers alongside gentle, organic radii for structural containers.

### Radii Application Rules
- **Pill Geometry (Full Curve / `9999px`):** Used universally for all action buttons, status chips, segmented filter tabs, notification counters, and search capsules.
- **Structural Card Enclosures (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`):** Containers, photo masks, school level cards (SD, SMP, SMA), and feature modules utilize soft, rounded corners that prevent visual harshness.
- **Organic Leaf Badges:** Special botanical badges utilize asymmetric radii (e.g., `rounded-tl-2xl rounded-br-2xl rounded-tr-sm rounded-bl-sm`) to symbolize organic growth and institutional heritage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background Forest Green (`#174C3C`), text Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border radius `9999px`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Typography `label-lg`. Right-pointing chevron/arrow icon for narrative momentum. Hover translates arrow by `4px` with background shifting to `#10382D`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #174C3C`, text `#174C3C`. Hover fills with `rgba(23, 76, 60, 0.05)`.
- **Accent Admission Button:** Background Lime Accent (`#C4DC75`), text Dark Forest (`#10382D`), font weight `700`. Used strictly for "PPDB 2026/2027" conversion points.

### Cards & School Level Selectors
- **School Cards (SD, SMP, SMA):** White card base with `1.5rem` radius, crisp photography top window with subtle `0.75rem` radius inner framing. Includes an icon avatar container filled with Light Mint (`#E5EFE7`) housing a deep green iconography symbol. Footer includes text action link (`Lihat Detail →`) in `label-lg`.
- **Feature Cards:** Light Mint surface, borderless, with deep forest mono-line icons enclosed in white circular discs.

### Segmented Filter Tabs
- **Pill Container:** Embedded track with light muted sage or mint background.
- **Active State:** Full Forest Green pill with white text and a soft bounce transition.
- **Inactive State:** Transparent with muted sage text (`#6F927D`). Hover state reveals soft mint wash.

### Form Inputs & Interactive Fields
- **Fields:** Pill or rounded (`1rem`) white surfaces with `1.5px` border in `#6F927D` at 30% opacity. Label positioned above in `label-md` uppercase. Active focus introduces a full Forest Green outline with zero harsh glow.

### Floating Editorial Annotations & Stats
- **Statistic Displays:** Large tabular numericals in Manrope `headline-xl` (Bold) paired with small multi-line descriptive labels in `body-sm` below. Divided by delicate `1px` vertical sage borders.
- **Floating Callout Badges:** Rounded organic leaf cards placed over imagery borders showing student achievements, character values, or quotes in Newsreader Italic (`"Tempat tumbuhnya potensi terbaik"`).
